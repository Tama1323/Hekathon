> *"Blending visual storytelling and cinematic craft with interactive software and real-time computing."*

I am a **Visual Effects (VFX) Artist** with **6+ years of production experience** in high-end video editing, visual effects, compositing, and cinematic color grading. Throughout my career, I've mastered industry-standard tools like **Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Photoshop, and Lightroom**.

Currently, I am expanding my creative horizon into **Game Development, 3D Animation, and Software Engineering** — bridging the gap between artistic visual direction and technical programming (Technical Artistry).

* 🎬 **Primary Profession:** VFX Artist, Motion Designer & Colorist (6+ Years)

* 🚀 **Currently Learning:** Game Architecture (C# / C++), 3D Modeling & Rigging (Blender), and Procedural Visuals
* 💡 **Open For:** Creative Tech collaborations, Indie Game projects, VFX supervision, and Technical Artist roles
